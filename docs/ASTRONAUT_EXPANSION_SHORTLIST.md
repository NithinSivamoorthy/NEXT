## Commercial terms and approval limits

CC Attribution permits commercial adaptation/distribution with attribution; exact license text/version must be captured from the chosen package/listing before use. [Creative Commons BY 4.0 explanation](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) is general guidance, not proof of either listing's version.

[CGTrader terms, sections 20–21B](https://www.cgtrader.com/pages/terms-and-conditions) permit commercial modification and distribution within an incorporated product, prohibit standalone redistribution, and require reasonable measures against end-user extraction. “No AI” restricts machine-learning/training use. It does not prohibit rendering an asset inside a product that separately generates text with Gemini. A public raw GLB URL would need particular care; don't assume an ordinary web asset directory meets the incorporated-product requirement. Seller licensing does not automatically clear third-party suit logos or insignia.

## Expected Expo work after approval

For any selected candidate: inspect mesh/material/texture counts and animation contents; remove unused scene elements; bake unsupported materials into ordinary metallic/roughness PBR; keep visor response simple; target 1–2K textures and a modest mobile mesh; prepare a self-contained uncompressed GLB. These are proposed steps, not work already done. Compression extensions may require unavailable native decoders and must not be assumed compatible.

Smallest proposed config change: add `glb` to Expo's default Metro asset extensions while preserving the existing exact native `three` resolver. No dependency change is inherently required for Three's existing GLTFLoader, but the installed R3F/Expo image/file path must be validated against the actual asset in Expo Go. Expected compatibility is **conditional**, never established by a browser preview.

Approval needed before acquisition and before Metro support. An inspection approval for IvanPetrov is the lowest-geometry realistic lead found; it would not imply approval to integrate an unrigged T-pose or accept unresolved commercial terms. The current proxy remains explicitly temporary until an acceptable asset is approved.
